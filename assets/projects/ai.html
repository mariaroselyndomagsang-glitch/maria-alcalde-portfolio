MARIA ALCALDE · PRACTICE PROJECT / AI-ASSISTED STARTER

AI-assisted productivity workflow

A worked example: turn operational notes into an accurate daily report.

Fictional business: Sage & Bean Coffee · Not actual client work

01 · Gather
Collect verified notes without private data.
02 · Draft
Ask an AI tool to structure the notes.
03 · Verify
Check figures, claims and omissions manually.
04 · Deliver
Format the approved report and retain source notes.

Input: fictional daily notes

25 November: 18 orders received; 15 completed; 3 awaiting pickup. Four inquiries; 3 answered; 1 awaiting owner response. Tomorrow: confirm pickup times and follow up on the outstanding inquiry.

Example AI prompt

Using only the notes provided, write a short daily operations report with a summary, order status, customer inquiries and next actions. Do not invent data. Flag missing information. Keep the tone professional and concise.

Example output: daily operations report

Summary: 18 orders were received, of which 15 were completed and 3 await pickup.

Customer inquiries: Three of four inquiries were answered; one requires an owner response.

Next actions: Confirm pickup times for the three pending orders and follow up with the owner on the unresolved inquiry.

Information not supplied: Sales revenue and pickup deadlines.

Human verification

15 + 3 = 18 orders. 3 + 1 = 4 inquiries. No revenue or deadlines were invented. Confirm recipient, confidentiality, and wording before sending. AI-assisted drafts must not replace human approval.
